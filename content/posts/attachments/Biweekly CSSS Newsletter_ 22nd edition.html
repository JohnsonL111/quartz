💬FAS The Teaching Talks (TTT)
The FAS TTT, CSGSA (CS grad school association), and CSSS invite FAS faculty and students to participate in a conversation session: “Listening to the Students’ Voices”.

This is an informal conversation between faculty and students looking to hear your thoughts on the academic resources provided by SFU (such as office hours, labs, tutorials, peer tutoring, etc…), learn more about the resources that you think would help you thrive, and hear more about your experience and ideas on mentorship opportunities.

Come chat with us in TASC 9204, eat some pizza, and get a chance to win one of ten $20 giftcards🤑 generously funded by the FAS Dean’s office (note that gift cards are for current students only)!

Feel free to email csss-doee@sfu.ca if any questions come up
Registration (for pizza ordering): http://websurvey.sfu.ca/survey/436134713
Date and Time: Tuesday November 15th, 1:00PM-2:10PM
Location: TASC 9204 (virtual option available)

💻CS Spring 2023 Peer Tutoring Applications
The FAS has opened Peer Tutoring Applications for the upcoming Spring semester. If you are able to spare 3-4 hours a week, and are knowledgeable in any of the courses CMPT 120, 125, 130, 135, 225, 295, 300, or MACM 101, then becoming a tutor may be for you! By tutoring fellow students, not only can you strengthen your grasp of these courses, but you will also gain valuable experience in instructing, communication, and leadership.

Information And Application: https://www.sfu.ca/computing/current-students/undergraduate-students/student-resources/cs_peer_tutoring1.html
Application Deadline: Sunday, Dec. 4th
